# Noto Sans / Noto Sans Thai

`NotoSans-{Regular,Bold}.ttf` (Latin/digits/punctuation) and
`NotoSansThai-{Regular,Bold}.ttf` (Thai script) are bundled locally so the
app never depends on fetching fonts from a CDN at runtime. `AppTheme` uses
NotoSans as the primary font family with NotoSansThai as
`fontFamilyFallback`, matching how Noto is split upstream.
